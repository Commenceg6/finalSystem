# Intelligent Patient Diagnostic System (IPDS)

**An AI-powered healthcare decision-support system designed to assist in identifying potential medical conditions based on patient symptoms and medical history.**

## Overview

The Intelligent Patient Diagnostic System (IPDS) is an Artificial Intelligence-based healthcare project developed to support preliminary patient diagnosis through intelligent analysis of reported symptoms and medical history.

The project explores how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can be applied to healthcare to address challenges associated with delayed diagnosis, diagnostic inconsistencies and limited access to medical expertise.

IPDS aims to provide users with preliminary diagnostic insights, possible medical conditions and relevant health recommendations through an accessible and user-friendly interface.

The system is designed as a healthcare decision-support tool to complement, not replace, professional medical consultation.

## Problem Statement

Traditional healthcare diagnostic processes can experience several challenges:

- Delayed diagnosis due to high patient volumes and limited medical resources.
- Diagnostic inconsistencies resulting from incomplete patient information.
- Limited access to specialized medical expertise.
- Difficulty analysing patient symptoms alongside historical medical information.
- Increased healthcare costs associated with delayed or inaccurate diagnosis.

IPDS explores the application of intelligent technologies to help address these challenges.

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

1. To develop a structured medical database containing diseases, associated symptoms, medical history and possible treatment information.
2. To develop an intelligent diagnostic system capable of analysing patient symptoms and medical history.
3. To evaluate the system's functionality, usability and diagnostic performance.

## Key Features

- **Symptom Analysis:** Allows users to submit symptoms for preliminary diagnostic assessment.
- **AI-Assisted Diagnosis:** Applies intelligent algorithms to identify potential medical conditions.
- **Medical History Consideration:** Incorporates patient medical information to support diagnostic analysis.
- **Diagnostic Recommendations:** Presents potential conditions and relevant health guidance.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Provides an accessible interface for interacting with the diagnostic system.
- **Patient Feedback:** Supports feedback collection to inform future system improvements.
- **Data Management:** Uses a structured approach to organising patient information and diagnostic records.
- **Security and Privacy:** Considers patient confidentiality, data protection and controlled access.

## System Architecture

The proposed system follows a modular architecture consisting of the following components:

1. **User Interface:** Handles patient interaction and symptom submission.
2. **Symptom Input Module:** Captures and validates patient symptoms.
3. **Medical History Module:** Retrieves relevant patient medical information.
4. **AI Diagnostic Engine:** Processes available information to identify potential conditions.
5. **Diagnostic Output Module:** Presents diagnostic insights and recommendations.
6. **Feedback Module:** Collects user feedback for system evaluation and improvement.
